In this story

    This series maps the path a single idea takes as it travels from the kernel to the person holding the device. It is a compact field guide to the conversations happening at every layer of software.

    Great engineers are great translators. We spend our time turning intent into instructions, and the only way to do that well is to communicate clearly with both machines and people.

    These notes outline the checkpoints we will revisit in the rest of the series:

    • Know who you are talking to at each layer of the stack.
    • Trace how data crosses the boundaries between hardware, kernel space, and user space.
    • Shape interfaces that keep the story intact as it climbs the stack.
    • Reflect feedback from the user back into the system.
